# Script and storyboard review rubric

Score each category from 0–5, then calculate the weighted score. This is a structured editorial conversation, not a prediction of watch time or reach.

| Category | Weight | Review questions |
|---|---:|---|
| Opening hook | 20 | Is the interesting action/contradiction visible immediately? Is the question specific? |
| Curiosity and stakes | 15 | Does the viewer know what they are waiting to learn and why it matters? |
| Pacing and progression | 15 | Does each beat add information or proof? Are pauses and transitions purposeful? |
| Visual proof | 20 | Does every major claim have a readable, correctly sourced visual? |
| Clarity and factual accuracy | 20 | Are terms, numbers, assumptions and limitations precise? |
| Payoff | 10 | Is the opening question resolved with one memorable takeaway? |

## Optional follower-conversion review

Keep this separate from the retention and technical-accuracy scorecards. Check: intended audience is clear; RaceXplained identity is reinforced; the reason to follow is credible; any series connection is useful; the CTA is appropriate; it begins after the payoff; it is concise; future-content promises are verified; it does not compete with captions or diagrams; and no CTA is the better ending. Return actionable feedback, not a precise follower prediction.

`weighted_score = Σ(category_score / 5 × weight)` → 100 points maximum.
