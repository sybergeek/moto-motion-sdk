# Moto Motion agent guidance

Use [docs/repository_structure.md](docs/repository_structure.md) as the source
of truth for public versus local files and for placing new code, examples,
scripts, assets, and documentation.

When planning, scripting or reviewing a RaceXplained reel, read [`docs/creative/retention_playbook.md`](docs/creative/retention_playbook.md) and use the templates under [`docs/creative/`](docs/creative/).

For future reels, also read [`docs/creative/follower_conversion_playbook.md`](docs/creative/follower_conversion_playbook.md). Treat conversion as an optional editorial decision: define the audience and credible reason to return, verify any future or related-content reference, and do not append a CTA automatically.

Before a future reel's full production render, use the Creative Quality Gate documented in [`docs/creative/creative_quality_gate.md`](docs/creative/creative_quality_gate.md): generate or select a hook, inspect the opening preview and first frame at mobile size, resolve objective findings, and record explicit approval for the exact creative revision. Use the documented bypass only for tests, debug renders, legacy workflows, or an explicit creator-approved exception.

The playbook is an editorial quality layer. It must not rewrite approved narration or silently change an approved topic. Renderer work begins only after editorial approval of the script, visual-narration map and factual checks.

When creating or reviewing a RaceXplained carousel, also read
[`docs/creative/carousel_generation.md`](docs/creative/carousel_generation.md)
and [`docs/creative/carousel_art_direction.md`](docs/creative/carousel_art_direction.md).
Apply their image-led composition, translucent overlay, shared slide-system,
contrast, authenticity, and rendered visual-QA defaults before exporting.
